About Mujahid Abbas

A developer focused on products that work reliably, look clear and keep growing.

Mujahid Abbas is a freelance AI web and mobile app developer from Pakistan. He builds Laravel web platforms, Flutter mobile apps and custom SaaS products for clients worldwide.

I work with businesses that have a clear idea and need it built properly. That usually means a secure backend, a clean interface and an app that stays fast as more people use it.

My main tools are Laravel, PHP and MySQL for the web, Flutter for Android and iOS, and WordPress or WooCommerce when a content site or store is the better fit. I also add AI features such as assistants and smart search when they solve a real problem.

Every project starts with a short conversation about goals and users, so we build the right thing first.
